Many platforms are now at the stage where scale brings a different set of payment challenges. Larger transaction volumes, more markets, and multi-party flows have made these businesses outgrow their initial all-in-one payment provider and move towards a multi-PSP setup. Managing multiple payment partners is technically difficult.
